ARTIST 01

PLAYBOI CARTI

Playboi Carti, born Jordan Terrell Carter on September 13, 1995, is a rapper from Atlanta, Georgia. He became known for his experimental sound, unique voice, energetic performances, and influence on modern hip-hop. His albums include Playboi Carti, Die Lit, Whole Lotta Red, and MUSIC. He is also the founder of the Opium record label.

BIRTHPLACEAtlanta, Georgia
BORNSeptember 13, 1995
LABELOpium

ARTIST 02

KEN CARSON

Ken Carson, born Kenyatta Lee Frazier Jr. on April 11, 2000, is a rapper from Atlanta, Georgia. He is signed to Opium and is known for his energetic performances, distorted production, and rage-inspired sound. His projects include Project X, X, A Great Chaos, and More Chaos. He has built a strong following through his unique style and collaborations with other artists.

BIRTHPLACEAtlanta, Georgia
BORNApril 11, 2000
