ARTIST 01

PLAYBOI CARTI

THE SOUND OF A NEW GENERATION

Playboi Carti, whose real name is Jordan Terrell Carter, was born on September 13, 1995, in Atlanta, Georgia. He is known for his unique voice, experimental beats, and influence on modern rap. His projects include Die Lit, Whole Lotta Red, and MUSIC. He is also the founder of the Opium label, which includes artists such as Ken Carson.

ARTIST 02

KEN CARSON

CHAOS WITH A DIFFERENT SOUND

Ken Carson, whose real name is Kenyatta Lee Frazier Jr., was born on April 11, 2000, in Atlanta, Georgia. He is a rapper signed to Opium and is known for his energetic sound and futuristic style. His projects include Project X, X, A Great Chaos, and More Chaos.

HOMETOWN

ATLANTA, GEORGIA

Atlanta has played a major role in shaping modern hip-hop. It is the hometown of both Playboi Carti and Ken Carson, and its music scene has influenced their sounds and styles.

MUSIC FESTIVAL

ROLLING LOUD

Rolling Loud is a major hip-hop festival featuring artists from around the world. Its large crowds, live performances, and festival atmosphere make it an important event for rap fans.

Summer Smash is a hip-hop music festival held in the Chicago area. It brings together rap artists and fans for a weekend of performances and music culture.
