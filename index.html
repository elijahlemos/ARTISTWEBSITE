THE KING OF VAMP

PLAYBOI CARTI

Playboi Carti, born Jordan Terrell Carter on September 13, 1995, is a rapper from Atlanta, Georgia. He became known for his experimental sound, unique voice, dark visuals, and influence on modern hip-hop.

His projects include Die Lit, Whole Lotta Red, and MUSIC. His sound continues to evolve through distorted production, aggressive vocals, and a distinctive live-performance style.

KEN CARSON

Ken Carson, born Kenyatta Lee Frazier Jr. on April 11, 2000, is a rapper from Atlanta, Georgia. He is signed to Opium and is known for his energetic sound, electronic production, and futuristic style.

His projects include Project X, X, A Great Chaos, and More Chaos. Ken has developed his own sound while helping shape the next generation of Opium-inspired hip-hop.
